# DevFest Atlantide 2027 — Practical information

- Venue: Palais des Abysses, 12 quai des Marées, Saint-Nazaire
- Dates: 14 and 15 October 2027
- Doors open at 08:15. The opening keynote starts at 09:00 in the Nautilus room.

## Getting there

A shuttle leaves Saint-Nazaire train station every 20 minutes from 07:30, and
runs until 20:00 on both days. Parking is available under the venue, 6 € a day.

## On site

- Wi-Fi network: `atlantide-2027`, password `bathyscaphe`
- Lunch is served in the Coral hall from 12:15 to 14:00, vegetarian by default
- The quiet room is on the first floor, next to the cloakroom
