# DevFest Atlantide 2027 — Programme

## Compiling for the Quantum Edge

- Speaker: Maya Okonkwo
- Room: Nautilus
- Slot: Thursday 14 October, 09:30 – 10:20

How to target quantum co-processors from a classical toolchain, and why the
usual optimisation passes have to be rewritten from scratch.

## Zero-Copy Serialisation in Kotlin Multiplatform

- Speaker: Tomas Lindqvist
- Room: Coral
- Slot: Thursday 14 October, 11:00 – 11:50

Moving structured data between the JVM and native targets without allocating,
and the three benchmarks that convinced the team it was worth the complexity.

## Observability for Batch Pipelines

- Speaker: Amara Diallo
- Room: Nautilus
- Slot: Friday 15 October, 15:00 – 15:50

Traces and metrics make sense for request-response systems. For a nightly batch
that runs for six hours, everything has to be reinvented.
